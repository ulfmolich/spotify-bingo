One name per line — line 1 is Card 1, line 2 is Card 2, and so on. Leave a line empty for an unassigned card. The same name on several cards is fine. You can also paste lines like 12, Anna to assign by card number (cards you don’t mention keep their name).

Playlist & cards
1 · Playlist

Export a public playlist with the Spotify Playlist/Song Exporter and load the CSV here (or drop the file on this window). The export has no release year, so years are filled in from songs the app already knows, then looked up on MusicBrainz (earliest release; needs internet, about one song per second). Looked-up years are tinted blue — worth a quick check. Anything not found stays highlighted for you to type in. Artist, title and year can all be edited.
